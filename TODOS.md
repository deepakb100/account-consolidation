## TODO-3: Add transaction_status field to handle refunds and reversals

**What:** Add `transaction_status TEXT DEFAULT 'cleared'` to the `transactions` table. Values: `'cleared'`, `'refunded'`, `'failed'`, `'pre_auth'`. When a refund email is parsed, look for a matching original transaction (same bank_account_id, same amount, close date) and mark original as `'refunded'`.

**Why:** ICICI and HDFC send distinct emails for refunds, pre-auth holds, and failed transactions. Without status, a ₹5,000 refund appears as a positive credit with no link to the original debit — inflating "savings" totals over time.

**Pros:** Accurate savings total; clean audit trail; refund detection is actually useful.
**Cons:** Matching original transaction from refund email is heuristic (amount+date+account — not always unique). Some false matches possible.

**Context:** Not a day-1 blocker — won't surface until you have a month of real data. Track it now so the schema change is planned (and covered by the migrations setup in TODO-2).

**Depends on:** TODO-2 (migrations must exist before adding a column to a live DB).
